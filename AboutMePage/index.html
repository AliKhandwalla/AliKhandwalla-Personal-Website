About Me

I'm studying for the AMC 12

The AMC 12 is a math competition for high school students.

Currently, I'm studying for it by practicing problems from previous years and learning new concepts in mathematics.

Some cool things about the AMC 12:

  • It is the first step to qualify for the AIME, USAMO, and other advanced math competitions.
  • The competition consists of 25 multiple-choice questions to be solved in 75 minutes.
  • It is held annually in November.
  • For more info, check out the official website: AMC 12 Official Website
